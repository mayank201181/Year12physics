# Year 12–13 IB Diploma Physics (SL & HL) — Vanishree's Hub

Interactive zero-login study guide, IB Data Booklet & mark-saver vault, procedural skill drill engine, interactive physics explorables, Scientific Investigation (IA) guide, and mock exam hub for **IB Diploma Physics (First Assessment 2025)** at Tanglin Trust School, Singapore.

## Highlights

- **Zero-Login Open Access + Automatic iPad `localStorage` Persistence** (`y12physics.*`, default learner `"Vanishree"`), with instant **SL | HL** toggle in the top bar.
- **10 Complete FA2025 IB Physics Topics across Themes A–E** (`A.1–E.5`), with HL-only topics and sections explicitly tagged (`level: "HL"`):
  - **Theme A:** `A.1–A.3` Mechanics · `A.4–A.5` Rigid Body Mechanics & Special Relativity *(HL)*
  - **Theme B:** `B.1–B.4` Thermal Transfers, Greenhouse, Gases & Thermodynamics *(B.4 HL)* · `B.5` Current & Circuits
  - **Theme C:** `C.1–C.2` SHM & Wave Model *(C.1 HL ext)* · `C.3–C.5` Wave Phenomena, Standing Waves & Doppler *(C.3/C.5 HL ext)*
  - **Theme D:** `D.1–D.3` Gravitational, Electric & Magnetic Fields *(potentials HL)* · `D.4` Electromagnetic Induction *(HL)*
  - **Theme E:** `E.1–E.2` Atomic Structure & Quantum Physics *(E.2 HL)* · `E.3–E.5` Radioactive Decay, Fission, Fusion & Stars *(E.3 decay law HL)*
- **📘 IB Data Booklet, Command Terms & Mark-Savers (`/formulas`)**: Official equations, SI constants, uncertainty propagation, command-term definitions, and *Never Write ❌ → Write Instead ✅* examiner swaps.
- **🧪 Scientific Investigation IA Guide (`/ia`)**: 2025 4×6-mark criteria (Research Design, Data Analysis, Conclusion, Evaluation), 3,000-word cap, Tanglin DP1–DP2 schedule, exemplar structure, moderator traps, and persistent checklist.
- **📝 FA2025 Mock Papers (`/exam`)**: Paper 1A (MCQ), Paper 1B (Data-based & experimental skills), Paper 2 (SL) and Paper 2 (HL) — aligned strictly to First Assessment 2025 (no Paper 3).
- **🎯 Procedural Skill Drills (`/skills`) & 60s Fluency Sprint (`/sprint`)**: Seeded adaptive drills across 3 tiers with SI unit and confusable-term checking.
